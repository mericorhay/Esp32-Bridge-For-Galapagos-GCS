Galapagos Bridge

Turn your ESP32 into a WiFi bridge for your telemetry radio. No Arduino IDE, no terminal, no drivers.

Supported boards

ESP32 ESP32-S3 ESP32-C3
  1. Plug the ESP32 into your computer with a USB cable.
  2. Click the button below. In the popup pick USB, select your board, and let it flash.
  3. Wire the radio (TX, RX, 5V, GND) to the ESP32, power it from a power bank, and join the Galapagos-Bridge WiFi.
  4. Open Galapagos and tap Connect. Done.

Runs locally in your browser via Web Serial — the page never uploads anything anywhere but your ESP32.
